1. MyDyson account

A lamp ignores every command until it is paired, and its key is issued by Dyson against the account the lamp belongs to. The lamp must already be registered in the MyDyson app. Pairing happens once; after it the plugin works entirely offline.

Two-letter code for the country your Dyson account belongs to.
Your lights already have their keys and will work without this. Authorising again is only needed to add a light or re-pair one.

2. Your lights

Lists the lights on your Dyson account, then scans for their Bluetooth addresses. Takes about 15 seconds. Your account is what identifies a light — over Bluetooth a light and a robot vacuum look identical.
